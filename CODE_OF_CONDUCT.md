# Code of conduct

We want CoreWatch to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and receive feedback on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers find their way around the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

Report problems privately to the maintainers through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/fintcore/corewatch/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the DynamiCore team. Reports are handled confidentially.
